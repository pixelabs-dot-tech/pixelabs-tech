How to play

  • Lira coin, +10The old Lebanese lira, cedar and all.
  • Phoenician coin, +150Rare, and usually high up. Double jump for it.
  • HelmetAn extra life. You start with one and can carry three.
  • TurboClears the road and speeds you up for a few seconds.
  • MagnetPulls nearby coins to you.
  • Lira ×2Every coin counts twice.
  • Potholes and open manholesJump them. They get wider as you speed up.
  • GoatsJump over them, or land on their back for a bounce.
  • Oncoming trafficA warning flashes before a car comes round the bend. Land on its roof to bounce.

Every village is about 15 seconds of riding, and the scenery follows its real elevation: snow above about 1,100 m, pine forest in the middle, and a view of the sea from the low villages.

Our curbside service

Nawilneناولني
na·wil·ne · Lebanese
“Hand it to me.”
At Jad Market it means you stay in the car. Pull up by the door, tell us what you need, and we bring it out to your window.

  1. Pull up by the door

    Stop outside the shop and stay in your seat.

  2. Tell us what you need

    Call, send a WhatsApp, or say it through the window.

  3. We hand it over

    Everything comes to your window. Pay and drive off.
